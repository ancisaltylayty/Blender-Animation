## ttuKeS5k4Uo: How Stitches Work (the most popular, no captions)
- **One continuous shot, zero cuts.** The camera starts on the calf below grey shorts, with a white sneaker and the blue backdrop behind.
- 0–4: the thread draws itself through a red gash. A new loop lands every 6–8 frames and the thread follows its own path.
- 4–8: the camera pushes in slowly and straight. Scissors slide in and snip the thread end.
- 8–16: still pushing in. The wound goes inflamed pink around the stitches, with shallow DOF and a blurred background.
- 16–22: macro view. Yellow "new tissue" fills the gap, then flat bright-red vessels grow through it like branching blobs.
- 22–24: the red fades to a pink-brown scar tissue colour.
- 24–31: an eased pull back to the whole calf. The stitches are gone and a faint scar remains. He starts walking, which resolves the story.
- **Lesson:** dolly in to explain and pull out to resolve, along one axis with no orbit. Biology is shown as flat, saturated, readable colours, not realism.

## Tutorial (_fIoJ_ywGfY): how a Claude + Blender recreation was built
- Measure everything: phase-correlation camera motion, luminance, backdrop RGB, loudness, words/s and transitions. Then run your own render through the same measurements. **The difference is the to-do list.**
- Camera: there is no orbit and no lateral drift. Use one dolly axis with every move eased. The first test had 8× the reference's jerk.
- Humans: use a rigged scan with bare skin where the story needs it. For expressions use 52 ARKit-style blend shapes, because a scan's jaw-only rig looks stiff.
- "If a real one exists, download it. Never build it by hand." That covers bones (anatomy database), Earth (NASA imagery), city (scanned facade kit) and the field (real diagram).
- Realism pass: softer lens, bloom, vignette, grain, motion blur on fast moves, matte skin with SSS, and a view transform that keeps the blue without clipping highlights.
- Sound: room tone in every world, hits on the exact frame of the matching word, music +3 dB and effects at half. The reference mix is loud with little dynamic range.
- Speed: render at 1080 and upscale, keep haze from acting as a light source, keep volumes in a fast mode, and run two Blender instances in parallel.
